# 001 · The First Question

**Series:** Before Fajr (salah)
**Brand:** Maktabatu Al-Ilm (brown #4D312C, orange #FB5606, white)
**World:** a fast, crowded day (shopping street, office, a phone-feed world, a wall of deadlines, a phone, an airport,
a bedroom, a car in traffic, a sofa) → the courtyard at night, from the hadith onward
**Character:** The Traveller (v7, brand colours: brown thobe, orange scarf, cream kufi)
**Target length:** 55-65 seconds
**Voice:** Kokoro am_adam, deep (pitch -2 semitones), speed 1.15: fast, urgent, close; very short gaps; a longer pause
before the hadith and after "And you've lost."
**Status:** final version (2026-11-19). The owner decided to publish; references.yaml still records the reviewer step.

---

## Accuracy rules for this script

- Text in **quotation marks** is an exact published translation (see `references.yaml`).
- Lines marked *(paraphrase)* explain a source in our own words. They are never shown in quotation marks on screen.
- Every source appears on screen while it is being narrated, and on the closing sources card.

## Script

### 1. Cold open · the three things
**Visual:** frame 1 already moving: a crash-zoom through the city at night into the Traveller. Three hard cuts, each in a
different place, each with things flying at the lens: a shopping street with glowing shop windows (coins), the office
(papers), a giant phone-feed world (notification bubbles).
**Narration:**
> Your money. Your job. Your followers.

### 2. The shatter
**Visual:** everything freezes, cracks and shatters toward the lens; a beat of silence; "first" slams on in orange.
**Narration:**
> None of it comes first.

### 3. The question
**Visual:** a dark space; the prayer tracker (clock + five dots) lights up above the Traveller.
**Narration:**
> There's one thing you'll be asked about before all of it.

### 4. The chase
**Visual:** a wall of calendars and sticky notes · phone POV flooded with pings · an airport departures board.
**Narration:**
> You chase deadlines. Notifications. Plans for next year.

### 5. Pushing it back
**Visual:** a fast time-lapse of one day (the clock running, the light changing). Bedroom ("one more minute"), a car in
traffic ("after this call"), the sofa with his phone ("one more video"): each beat cracks a tracker dot.
**Narration:**
> And every day, you push it back. One more minute. After this call. One more video.

### 6. The hadith
**Visual:** match cut, the phone's glow becomes the lamp's glow: the courtyard at night. The exact quote on screen with its
source tag.
**Narration:**
> But the Prophet ﷺ told us: "Indeed the first deed by which a servant will be called to account on the Day of Resurrection is his Salat."

**On screen:** the exact English translation, then the source tag: JAMI' AT-TIRMIDHI 413 · SAHIH (DARUSSALAM)

### 7. The weight of it
**Visual:** "succeeded" in green, "lost" in red; on "lost" the lamp is snuffed out; silence.
**Narration** *(paraphrase)*:
> If it's sound, you've succeeded.
> If it's lacking, you've failed. And you've lost.

### 8. The mercy
**Visual:** the lamp comes back; a swirl of small gold lights (voluntary prayers) rises and mends the cracked dots one by one.
**Narration** *(paraphrase)*:
> But Allah is merciful. If your obligatory prayers fall short, He will say: look, does My servant have voluntary prayers? And with them, what was missing is completed.

### 9. The turn
**Visual:** he walks to the prayer mat and raises his hands (takbir); the clock shows the next prayer.
**Narration:**
> So the question isn't whether you're perfect.
> It's whether you'll stand up for the next one.
> Your next prayer is closer than you think.

### 10. Ending
**Visual:** the camera cranes up; the floor pattern completes into the star mark. Then a 5-second quiet moment (no voice,
no effects, soft ambience only) for a recitation added on TikTok. Then the end card: the Maktabatu Al-Ilm logo and the
sources.
**On screen, end card:**
- Sources: Jami' at-Tirmidhi 413, graded Sahih (Darussalam)
