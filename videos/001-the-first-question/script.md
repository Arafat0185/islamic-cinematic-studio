## Script

### 1. Opening · 0:00–0:04
**Visual:** Total darkness. One oil lamp ignites; light runs along the star-tile floor. (Signature opening.)
**Sound:** A single water drop, then quiet night ambience.
**Narration:** *(none)*

### 2. Hook · 0:04–0:11
**Visual:** Slow push-in on the empty courtyard. The Traveller stands alone under the arches.
**Narration:**
> Before your money. Before your job. Before your followers.
> There is one thing you will be asked about first.

**On screen:** "asked about **first**" (key word in gold)

### 3. The chase · 0:11–0:21
**Visual:** Low angle behind the Traveller. Far away, beyond the arches, a city glows (phones, traffic lights, windows).
**Narration:**
> We spend our days chasing things that will never be mentioned on that Day.
> Deadlines. Notifications. Plans for next year.

### 4. The hadith · 0:21–0:33
**Visual:** The Traveller turns toward the lamp. The camera drifts down to a single prayer mat on the floor, lit by the lamp.
**Narration:**
> But the Prophet ﷺ told us what comes first:
> "Indeed the first deed by which a servant will be called to account on the Day of Resurrection is his Salat."

**On screen:** the exact English translation, then the source tag: JAMI' AT-TIRMIDHI 413 · SAHIH (DARUSSALAM)
**On screen (Arabic):** the opening of the hadith's Arabic text, copied verbatim from sunnah.com. [TODO: paste]

### 5. The weight of it · 0:33–0:43
**Visual:** Wide shot. The lamp flickers; the courtyard feels very large and the Traveller very small.
**Narration** *(paraphrase)*:
> He ﷺ said that if it is sound, the servant has succeeded,
> and if it is lacking, he has failed and lost.

**On screen:** "**succeeded**" in green, "**lost**" in red (keyword colour system)

### 6. The mercy · 0:43–0:57
**Visual:** Warm light grows. Small points of light (voluntary prayers) drift up from the floor pattern and fill gaps in the star tiles one by one, completing the pattern.
**Narration** *(paraphrase)*:
> But then comes the mercy.
> If something is missing from the obligatory prayers, Allah will say: look, does My servant have voluntary prayers?
> And with them, what was lacking is completed.

**On screen:** "voluntary prayers" in gold

### 7. The turn · 0:57–1:08
**Visual:** The Traveller walks to the prayer mat and raises his hands to begin the prayer (takbir pose). Slow push-in.
**Narration:**
> So the question isn't whether you're perfect.
> It's whether you'll stand up for the next one.
> Your next prayer is closer than you think.

### 8. Ending · 1:08–1:12
**Visual:** Camera rises above the courtyard; the floor pattern completes into the star mark. The lamp dims. (Signature ending.)
**On screen, sources card:**
- Jami' at-Tirmidhi 413, graded sahih (Darussalam)
- Quran 20:14 [only if used: see the optional line below]
